# Graphic, vector, and 3D delivery

Load only for requested brand assets, vector work, or 3D scenes.

## Composition and rights

Build an original composition from the product hierarchy and supplied brand. Attribute any external asset and verify redistribution terms; referencing a gallery does not permit copying its artwork.

Use a restrained set of effects when they support hierarchy. Effect counts, golden ratios, and negative-space percentages are local choices, not standards or universal bans.

## Vector acceptance

- Provide valid viewBox geometry and consistent optical weight.
- Check requested display sizes, including 16/24 px when delivering small UI marks.
- Test light/dark and monochrome uses required by the brief.
- Ensure accessible names for meaningful graphics and hide purely decorative graphics from assistive technology.
- Preserve editable sources and license notices for included assets.

Do not invent affiliation, trademarks, certifications, or client approval. Deliver only the variants actually needed by the brief.

## 3D acceptance

Record scene purpose, supported runtime, asset/texture budgets, loading/failure behavior, and a static alternative. Keep essential information and controls accessible outside the canvas.

Verify installed rendering-library APIs before coding. Dispose owned resources, bound pixel ratio, pause nonessential rendering when hidden, and profile on target devices. Camera, lighting, and material values depend on the scene; arbitrary numeric recipes do not establish physical accuracy.

Ground text alternatives, keyboard access, and pause controls in [WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/) 1.1.1, 2.1.1, and 2.2.2. A 3D preview or screenshot does not verify interaction accessibility.
