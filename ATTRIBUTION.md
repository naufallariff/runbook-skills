## Source-use rules

- Cite the specific source, version or section, and supported claim beside new mechanisms or in the skill's reference ledger.
- Synthesize original workflows. Do not copy or thinly paraphrase third-party repositories or commercial skill products.
- Linking a document does not relicense it. Check the applicable terms before including excerpts, code, assets, or fixtures; retain required notices and exclude material with unresolved rights.
- Preserve existing MIT copyright notices in `LICENSE`. Record additional notices where applicable.
- Distinguish normative standards, guidance, and local design choices. Do not imply affiliation, endorsement, certification, benchmark results, or a repository-wide compliance audit.
- Report source or licensing uncertainty explicitly; never invent provenance or verification.
